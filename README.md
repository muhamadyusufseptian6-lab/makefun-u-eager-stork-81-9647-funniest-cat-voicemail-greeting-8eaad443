One voicemail greeting, recorded by a cat. Mochi is currently asleep on your
clean laundry — do not disturb — and at 4 a.m. sharp he will knock your glass
off the table, then scream for tuna. That is the whole schedule. He sees no
reason to change it.

## The entry

> You've reached Mochi. I'm asleep on your clean laundry. At 4 a.m. I'll knock your glass off, then scream for tuna.

114 characters. One line. Zero apologies — Mochi has never once apologized
for anything, and he isn't about to start with your voicemail.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, fully deterministic) writes the
greeting to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. Nothing fetched, nothing random —
Mochi is predictable in exactly one way: the same run always produces the
same text, and the same cat always knocks the glass off.

```sh
node main.ts
```
